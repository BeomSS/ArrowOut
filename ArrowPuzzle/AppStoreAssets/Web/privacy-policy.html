Privacy Policy (English)

App: Arrow Out: Shape Puzzle · Effective Date: November 23, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Arrow Out: Shape Puzzle ("the App") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage analytics.

  • No account creation or login is required.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking identifiers (IDFA) are included.
  • The App operates offline and does not transmit data to external servers.

2. Local On-Device Storage

To preserve your gameplay experience, the App stores only your current level progress, active puzzle session, theme preference (Light/Dark), and sound/haptic settings locally on your device via Apple's UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects zero personal data from any user, it is safe for players of all ages.
